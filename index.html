How BidLive Works

Create an account, browse database-backed lots, and place a bid.

1
👤

Create an Account

Register as a buyer or seller. Passwords are hashed by the backend; identity verification is not available yet.

2
🔍

Find an Auction

Browse auction listings, follow countdown timers, review product details, and view stored bid histories.

3
⚡

Place Your Bid

Enter a bid or use quick increments. Bid updates are saved by the backend; push notifications are not available yet.

4
🏆

Win the Auction

The highest bid when an auction ends determines the result. Payment and fulfillment are not available yet.
